# Yuchen Wang

**Independent Researcher**

I work on AI systems that support scientific discovery and organizational learning. My current research interests include **AI for Science**, **scientific agents**, **enterprise AI**, and **organizational intelligence**.

## Current research

### ADS — AI for Scientific Discovery

ADS explores how AI systems can move from an existing knowledge state, K<sub>t</sub>, toward a defensible new knowledge state, K<sub>t+1</sub>. The work focuses on hypothesis generation, experiment planning and orchestration, evidence evaluation, and iterative knowledge revision, with materials science as the initial domain.

### Chera — Organizational Intelligence

Chera explores an enterprise-owned context, organizational state, and learning layer for collaboration between people and AI agents. It focuses on preserving evidence, authority, decisions, outcomes, and organizational memory across workflows and systems.
